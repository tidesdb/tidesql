This manual is the companion to the [TidesDB library manual](/preface). It is the TideSQL 5.x
manual for TidesDB v10.x.x. The reading order and navigation come from `manual.json`. The chapters:

- **Preface** - [Preface](/preface)
- **Getting Started** - [Installing TideSQL](/getting-started/install), [Your First Table](/getting-started/first-table)

- **Administration** - [Monitoring](/administration/monitoring), [Backup and Checkpoint](/administration/backup), [Table Maintenance](/administration/maintenance), [Online DDL](/administration/online-ddl), [Partitioning](/administration/partitioning)
- **SQL Reference** - [Table Options](/reference/table-options), [System Variables](/reference/system-variables), [Status Variables](/reference/status-variables), [Time-To-Live](/reference/ttl), [Data-at-Rest Encryption](/reference/encryption), [Full-Text Search](/reference/full-text-search), [Vector Search](/reference/vector-search), [Spatial Indexes](/reference/spatial-indexes), [Generated Columns and JSON](/reference/generated-columns-and-json)
- **Internals** - [How Data Is Stored](/internals/storage), [Statistics and the Optimizer](/internals/optimizer), [Write-Path Optimizations](/internals/write-path)
- **Appendixes** - [Limitations](/appendix/limitations), [MariaDB Compatibility](/appendix/compatibility), [Glossary](/appendix/glossary)
